About Me


Hello, I'm Akhila! I'm a junior at UTD studying Information Technology. I love making art in my free time and so I've decided to make this little art blog to yap about it. I really video games too so I make a ton of fan art. Some of my favorite games that you might see me make art of are NieR, Ace Attorney, Yakuza, Final Fantasy 7, Persona, and more!

I've been doing art ever since I was a little kid and my mom is an artist too so it runs in the family. I've entered a few exhibitions and even won an award before. I always used to recycle old things to make art because I had a hard time asking my parents to buy me stuff so a lot of the art I do is pretty affordable!

This blog is sort of going to be like a portfolio/social media sort of thing for me since I don't like actual social media. So basically, it's just gonna be me talking about the processes behind my art and my thoughts on each piece. If you ever want to reach me, feel free to contact me through the contact page on this site!
